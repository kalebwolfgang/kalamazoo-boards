Civil Rights Board

Reviews claims of discrimination brought by City residents under the Non-Discrimination and Fair Housing Ordinances, and makes recommendations for resolution after investigation by the City Manager. Also recommends ways to improve City programs and ordinances to eliminate discrimination. Established September 2020 under Chapter 18 of the Code of Ordinances.
